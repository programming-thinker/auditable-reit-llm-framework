On September 15, 2020, Digital Realty announced that it will redeem all 10,000,000 shares of its 5.875% Series G Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock (par value $0.01 per share) (the “Series G Preferred Stock) on October 15, 2020 (the “Redemption Date”). The redemption price for the Series G Preferred Stock is equal to $25.00 per share, plus all accrued and unpaid dividends on such shares up to but not including the Redemption Date, in an amount equal to $0.057118 per share, for a total payment of $25.057118 per share. The previously announced dividend of $0.367188 per share of Series G Preferred Stock will be paid on September 30, 2020 to holders of record as of the close of business on September 15, 2020. This redemption is made at the option of the company pursuant Section 5 of the Articles Supplementary establishing and fixing the rights and preferences of the Series G Preferred Stock. Series G Preferred Stock held through the Depository Trust Company will be redeemed in accordance with the applicable procedures of the Depository Trust Company. In connection with the redemption, on the Redemption Date, the operating partnership will redeem all 10,000,000 units of its Series G Preferred Units held by Digital Realty Trust, Inc.
