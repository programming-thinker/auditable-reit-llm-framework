Election of Director Equinix, Inc. (“Equinix”) has elected Jeetu Patel and Fidelma Russo as independent directors to its Board of Directors (the “Board”), effective June 9, 2022. With the election of Mr. Patel and Ms. Russo, Equinix’s Board now consists of eleven members. Mr. Patel will serve as a member of the Board’s Nominating and Governance Committee and Ms. Russo will serve as a member of the Audit Committee. As members of Equinix’s Board, Mr. Patel and Ms. Russo will receive Equinix’s standard compensation for non-employee directors. Item 8.01 Other Events On June 13, 2022, Equinix issued a press release announcing the election of Mr. Patel and Ms. Russo to the Board.
