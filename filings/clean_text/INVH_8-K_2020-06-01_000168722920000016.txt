The Investor Presentation referred to in Item 7.01 above includes, among other things, certain preliminary estimated operating data for April and May 2020 and certain liquidity information as of April 30, 2020. The Company’s rent collection rate in May 2020 was 97% of historical average, up from 95% in April 2020. Same Store average occupancy increased to a record-high 97.5% in May 2020, representing an increase of 90 basis points as compared to May 2019 and an increase of 30 basis points from April 2020. The Company’s blended lease-over-lease rent growth was 3.4% in May 2020, up from 3.2% in April 2020. As of April 30, 2020, the Company had liquidity of approximately $1.1 billion through a combination of unrestricted cash and undrawn capacity on its credit facility and no debt maturing prior to 2022. In May 2020, the Company repaid $120 million of indebtedness under its revolving facility.
