American Tower Corporation (the “Company”) is hereby providing certain information about its business in light of the U.S. Department of Justice’s approval of the merger between T-Mobile US, Inc. (“T-Mobile US”) and Sprint Corporation (“Sprint”). For the quarter ended March 31, 2019, T-Mobile US and Sprint accounted for approximately 10% and 8%, respectively, of the Company’s consolidated property revenues. For the quarter ended March 31, 2019, on sites where both companies had separate leases for antenna space, the revenue generated from each of T-Mobile US and Sprint represented approximately 4% of the Company’s consolidated property revenues, including revenue from legacy Metro PCS and Clearwire leases. The average remaining non-cancellable current term of the leases closest to expiration on these sites with T-Mobile US and Sprint is between two and three years. The information in this Current Report on Form 8-K is being furnished pursuant to Item 7.01 of Form 8-K and shall not be deemed “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (“Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall such information be deemed incorporated by reference in any filing under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as shall be expressly set forth by specific reference in such a filing.
