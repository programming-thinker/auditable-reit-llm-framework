After deducting the Initial Purchasers commissions and estimated offering expenses, the Operating Partnership received approximately $489.7 million in net proceeds in connection with the issuance and sale of the Notes. The Notes are general unsecured senior obligations of the Operating Partnership and rank equally in right of payment with all other senior unsecured indebtedness of the Operating Partnership. Interest will accrue from September 21, 2015 and is payable semiannually in arrears on April 1 and October 1 of each year, beginning on April 1, 2016 until the maturity date of October 1, 2035. The Operating Partnerships obligations under the Notes are fully and unconditionally guaranteed by the Company.
