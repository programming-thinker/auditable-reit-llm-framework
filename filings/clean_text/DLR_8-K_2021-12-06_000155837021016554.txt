Other Events. On December 6, 2021, Digital Realty announced the completion of the listing of Digital Core REIT as a standalone real estate investment trust publicly traded on the Main Board of Singapore Exchange Securities Trading Limited. Digital Core REIT is sponsored and externally managed by Digital Realty. In connection with the listing, Digital Realty contributed a 90% interest in a portfolio of 10 assets concentrated in major data center markets across the U.S. and Canada valued at approximately $1.4 billion. Digital Realty is retaining a 10% direct ownership stake in each of the assets in the initial Digital Core REIT portfolio and will own an initial approximately 35% ownership interest in Digital Core REIT, assuming exercise of the overallotment option. Digital Realty will earn fees for asset and property management as well as acquisitions, dispositions and development.
