On December 16, 2019, American Tower Corporation (the “Company”) issued a press release (the “Press Release”) announcing its election to call for redemption all of its outstanding 5.900% senior unsecured notes due 2021 (the “5.900% Notes”). In accordance with the redemption provisions of the 5.900% Notes and the Indenture, dated as of May 13, 2010 (the “Base Indenture”), as supplemented by the Supplemental Indenture No. 3, dated as of October 6, 2011 (“Supplemental Indenture No. 3” and, together with the Base Indenture, the “5.900% Indenture”) between the Company and the Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A., as trustee, the 5.900% Notes will be redeemed at a price equal to the principal amount of the 5.900% Notes plus a make-whole premium calculated pursuant to the terms of the 5.900% Indenture, together with accrued and unpaid interest, if any, up to, but excluding, the redemption date, which has been set for January 15, 2020.
