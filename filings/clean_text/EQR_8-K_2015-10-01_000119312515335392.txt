A new Section 16 of Article II of the Bylaws now permits a shareholder, or group of up to 20 shareholders, owning 3% or more of the Companys outstanding common shares continuously for at least 3 years, to include in the Companys annual meeting proxy materials trustee nominations for up to 20% of the seats on the Companys Board of Trustees.
