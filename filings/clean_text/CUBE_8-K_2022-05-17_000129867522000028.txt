The Company also expects to enter into similar indemnification agreements with its future trustees and executive officers. The Indemnification Agreements replace and supersede previous indemnification agreements between the Company and each of its trustees and executive officers. The changes to the previous indemnification agreements reflected in the new Indemnification Agreements are administrative in nature.
