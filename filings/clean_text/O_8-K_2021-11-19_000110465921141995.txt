Nugent, the current Senior Vice President, Controller of Realty Income Corporation (the “Company”), has been appointed as the Company’s Senior Vice President, Controller and Principal Accounting Officer, effective January 1, 2022. Mr. Nugent, 49, has served as the Company’s Senior Vice President, Controller since 2017, and as the Company’s interim Principal Financial Officer and Treasurer from January 2020 to January 2021. Mr. Nugent is a licensed Certified Public Accountant in California. Mr. Nugent holds a B.S. in Accounting and a minor in Math from the University of San Diego. Mr. Nugent has no family relationships with any director, executive officer or person nominated or chosen by the Company to become a director or executive officer of the Company.
