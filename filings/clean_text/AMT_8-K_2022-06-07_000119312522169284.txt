The offering resulted in net proceeds to the Company of approximately $2,291.9 million, after reflecting underwriting discounts and estimated offering expenses. The Company intends to use the net proceeds from this offering to repay existing indebtedness under its $3.0 billion 364-day U.S. Dollar-denominated unsecured term loan entered into in December 2021, including borrowings thereunder that were used to finance its acquisition of CoreSite Realty Corporation, which closed in December 2021.
