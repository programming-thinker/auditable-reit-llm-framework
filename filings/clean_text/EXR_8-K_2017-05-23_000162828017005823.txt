The election of seven members of the Company’s board of directors for terms expiring at the 2018 annual meeting of stockholders and until their successors are duly elected and qualify. Proposal 2. The ratification of the engagement of Ernst & Young LLP as the Company’s independent registered public accounting firm for 2017. Proposal 3. The approval, on an advisory basis, of the compensation of the Company's named executive officers, as disclosed in the Company’s proxy statement. Proposal 4. The approval, on an advisory basis, of the frequency of stockholder votes on executive compensation.
