With the addition of two new independent directors to the Board of AvalonBay Communties, Inc. (the Company) in the last year, Bruce Choate and the Board decided on March 3, 2015, after review and consultation, that Mr. Choate will not stand for re-election at the upcoming 2015 Annual Meeting of Shareholders. Mr. Choate has been a director of the Company for over 20 years, since the initial public offering of the Company. He has served, at various times, on the Audit, Compensation, Investment and Finance, and Nominating and Corporate Governance committees of the Board. Mr. Choate has been and continues to be a valued member of the Board, and the decision that he not stand for re-election was based on a review of the composition and succession plan of the Board and not on any disagreement with Mr. Choate.
