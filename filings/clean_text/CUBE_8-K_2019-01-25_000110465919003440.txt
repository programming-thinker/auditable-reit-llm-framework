The foregoing is not a complete description of the Underwriting Agreement and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of the Underwriting Agreement attached to this Current Report on Form 8-K as Exhibit 1.1 and incorporated herein by reference. Certain of the Underwriters and/or their affiliates act as lenders and/or agents under the Companys credit facility and those lenders therefore may receive a portion of the proceeds from the offering of the Notes and related Guarantees through the repayment of those borrowings.
