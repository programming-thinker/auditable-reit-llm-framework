The purpose of the Omnibus Plan is to attract, retain and motivate participating employees and to attract and retain well-qualified individuals to serve as members of the board of directors, consultants and advisors through the use of incentives based upon the value of the Companys common stock. Awards under the Omnibus Plan may consist of incentive awards, stock options, stock appreciation rights, performance shares, performance units, shares of common stock, restricted stock, restricted stock units or other stock-based awards as determined by the administrator of the Omnibus Plan. The Omnibus Plan provides that an aggregate maximum of 7,000,000 shares of our common stock are reserved for issuance under the Omnibus Plan, subject to adjustment for certain corporate events. This number includes 3,000,000 shares previously approved by the shareholders. The number of shares reserved for issuance will be depleted on the grant date of an award by the maximum number of shares of common stock, if any, that may be issued under such award. Awards that may only be settled in cash will not deplete the number of shares reserved under the Omnibus Plan. The Omnibus Plan was filed as Appendix B to the Companys definitive proxy statement filed March 21, 2019 and the terms thereof are incorporated herein by reference.
